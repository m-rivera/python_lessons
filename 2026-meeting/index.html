2026 Python in Chemistry Meeting

The 2026 Python in Chemistry Meeting will take place on at Manchester Metropolitan University on Wednesday 20th May. To register, please complete this form.

Goals of the Meeting¶

In discussions at the previous two Python in Chemistry Meetings, it has become clear that Python is being taught in different ways at different institutions. Partly this is a function of the mode and timing of delivery: whether it is delivered in core or optional modules; whether in a standalone module or as part of a broader skills module; to which year group(s); which applications are in focus. As a result of this variation in logistics, the goals, methods and structure of our Python teaching practice diverge. We aim to use this meeting to showcase some of these different approaches, and provide attendees an opportunity to dicuss with other educators how their teaching methods and materials could be adapted to suit the particular constraints and requirements of their institution.

Meeting details¶

The meeting will take place in the Dalton Building (DB 1.06) in the Chemistry Department of Manchester Metropolitan University on Wednesday 20th May, beginning at 11:00 and ending at 16:30. Lunch and other refreshments will be provided.

Attendance is free.

There are some funds available for travel costs; thanks to the RSC HE Group and the RSC Chemical Information and Computer Applications Group. If you would like support with travel costs and are unable to obtain them from your university or the RSC, please contact l.munro@mmu.ac.uk or miguel.rivera@ucl.ac.uk.

Getting to MMU¶

The Dalton building is just off of Mancunian Way (A57), and is marked with “DB” on the MMU campus map.

By Train: The Dalton Building is about a 5 minute walk from the Manchester Oxford Road or 15 minutes from Manchester Piccadilly station. You are encouraged to travel by train if possible!

By Car: If you are driving then the address is Dalton Building, Chester Street, Manchester, M1 5GD. If you need a car parking space, please indicate on the sign up form, and we will attempt to book in the Manchester Met Dalton Building Car Park. There are limited spaces and they can only be reserved with the driver / licence plate details at a cost of £4.40 for a day. There is a SIP Car Park (Manchester Student Village) on Chester Street (£8 for 12 hours) and a NCP Car Park on Chester Street (£20!!). For other car parks within a walkable distance of Dalton Building, see: Manchester Car Parks - Google My Maps.
